Key Rules

How It Works

The essential rules users need to understand about the program.

7 Days, 4 Weeks

The same 7 spiritual disciplines are repeated each week: Identity, Dominion, Word, Prayer, Holiness, Faith, and Purpose. Repetition builds lasting habits.

One Day at a Time

You cannot skip ahead. Each day unlocks only after the previous day is completed AND the next calendar day has begun. No rushing.

Daily Scripture

Each day opens with two Bible verses — a primary and a supporting verse. Read them aloud and meditate before answering questions.

Weekly Assessment

After completing all 7 days, answer 3 reflection questions. This advances you to the next week. Week 4 ends with a final graduation assessment.

Build Your Streak

The 7-day streak resets each week. Complete all 7 days faithfully, do your assessment, and start fresh the next week.

Journey Complete

After 4 weeks (28 days total), you graduate from the First Timers program. Walk in spiritual maturity and dominion!
